# ADR-0016 — A failed citation discards the whole answer

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-08-11

## Context

The product's headline claim is that every citation is real, was retrieved, and
was in force on the date asked about.

## Decision

Cite by opaque per-request integer ids. Validate every cited id against
existence, the retrieved set, and the admissible set. Any failure discards the
answer and returns `FABRICATED_CITATION`.

## Alternatives

- **Score citations and threshold** — a threshold that passes anything is not
  a guarantee. Rejected.
- **Strip the bad citation and show the rest** — leaves claims standing with
  nothing behind them; strictly worse than removing both the claim and its
  support. Rejected.
- **Show with a warning** — warnings are read by people who already doubt the
  output; this protects the user who trusts it. Rejected.

## Consequences

**Positive.** A hallucination costs a whole answer, which is the only cost
that changes a model's behaviour into a user-visible guarantee. Every shown
citation is individually provable.

**Negative.** One bad citation discards potentially correct content. The
`admissible` check is redundant given correct retrieval and is kept
deliberately: it re-asserts the product's central invariant at the boundary
where it becomes user-visible, independently of the code meant to enforce it.
