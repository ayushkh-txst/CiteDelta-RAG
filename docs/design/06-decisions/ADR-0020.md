# ADR-0020 — Conversational interface with a temporal gutter

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-08-13

## Context

Time is the product but was presented as a form field; the corpus is 78
discrete amendment dates, not a continuum. The owner's verdict on the previous
UI was that none of it should survive.

## Decision

Rebuild as a conversation. No bubbles, avatars, or assistant persona. Each
turn is stamped with its own as-of date in a left gutter; a change in as-of
renders a labelled rupture across the record. Follow-ups are resolved from
natural language, making *"what about in 2019?"* the primary way to
time-travel.

## Alternatives

- **Keep the form and improve the date control** — still hides that the dates
  are discrete, and rules out follow-ups. Rejected.
- **Conventional chat bubbles** — the most templated UI available; for an
  AI-infra portfolio, "another RAG chatbot" is a liability. Rejected.

## Consequences

**Positive.** The conversation record makes the discrete timeline the
interface: the gutter stamps every turn, and the rupture turns a date change
into a visible event. The signature is something no other chat has.

**Negative.** Turns need conversation identity and history — one migration on
`query_traces` (conversation_id, turn_index, resolved_query), no new table.
Every turn now costs a resolver call once history exists (~3% overhead, see
ADR-0021). The interface is distinctive but unconventional, so nothing about
it is familiar to a first-time user; the composer placeholder carries the
teaching.
